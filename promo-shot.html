The Ridgeway in three days

Britain's oldest road runs 87 miles along the chalk downs. We split it into three manageable days with a pub at the end of each one.

Day one climbs out of Overton Hill past the sarsen stones, a steady five hours of wide green track with skylarks overhead. Carry more water than you think you need; the downs are dry country.

What to pack

One change of socks per day, a windproof layer, and the smallest first-aid kit you can assemble yourself. Skip the camping stove — this is pub-lunch territory.
